Frequently asked questions

What is Konfide In One's corporate wellness program?+
Konfide In One activates your existing corporate gym with on-site expert coaching across four integrated pillars: training, nutrition, recovery, and body composition measurement. No new equipment or app required — just the team to run what you already built.
How does the on-site wellness program work for companies?+
Every engagement starts with a functional movement assessment. From there, clients receive on-site personal coaching, group classes, nutrition coaching, recovery services and quarterly body composition tracking — all delivered directly at your corporate gym.
Do we need to buy new equipment to start?+
No. Konfide In One activates the gym you already have. The goal is to turn an underused fitness amenity into a measurable wellness program with zero new equipment and no new app for employees.
Who founded Konfide In One?+
Konfide In One was founded by an enterprise sales veteran with over a decade of experience at major technology companies, bringing deep expertise in corporate operations, scaling and client ecosystems.
