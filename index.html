FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Konfide In One?+
Konfide In One is an on-site wellness company based in Austin, TX. One trusted team unifies training, nutrition, recovery and body composition measurement into a single accountable system, delivered directly where you live and work.
How does the unified model work?+
Every client starts with a functional movement assessment. From there, four pillars — Train, Fuel, Restore, Measure — run as one connected system so nothing is left to guesswork.
Who is Konfide In One for?+
Three audiences: companies with an underused gym, luxury residential properties and HOA boards, and individual high performers who value their time.
Where do sessions take place?+
All coaching and recovery services are delivered on site — at your corporate gym or residential building. There are zero commutes; everything comes to you.
