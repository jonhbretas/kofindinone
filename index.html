Why I built this

I kept meeting people who were winning at everything except their own body.

Founders. Executives. People with no shortage of discipline. What they were missing was someone in their corner who actually knew them — their history, their schedule, the injury they never dealt with, the reason they stopped last time.
Almost everything traces back to a root cause. Gut health, fatigue, brain fog, a nagging injury — you have to start at the foundation to actually solve it. Most wellness stops at “just train harder.” I wanted to build something that goes deeper.
So I built the thing I wanted for myself: one team, one plan, and a coach who notices when you go quiet for a week. Everything under one roof, coordinated, so you never fall through the cracks again.
“Confidence in your health becomes confidence everywhere else. That’s the whole point.”

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Konfide In One?+
Konfide In One is an on-site wellness company based in Austin, TX. One trusted team unifies training, nutrition, recovery and body composition measurement into a single accountable system, delivered directly where you live and work.
How does the unified model work?+
Every client starts with a functional movement assessment. From there, four pillars — Train, Fuel, Restore, Measure — run as one connected system so nothing is left to guesswork.
Who is Konfide In One for?+
Three audiences: companies with an underused gym, luxury residential properties and HOA boards, and individual high performers who value their time.
Where do sessions take place?+
All coaching and recovery services are delivered on site — at your corporate gym or residential building. There are zero commutes; everything comes to you.
