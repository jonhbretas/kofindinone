FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is residential wellness at Konfide In One?+
Konfide In One brings a unified, on-site wellness program to luxury residential properties. Expert coaching, nutrition, recovery and measurement are delivered directly to residents where they live — no commute, no excuses.
Who is the residential wellness program for?+
It serves property managers, HOA boards and individual residents. For property managers and HOAs, KN1 activates the existing gym as a resident draw; for residents, it provides expert personal training, nutrition coaching, recovery services and quarterly body composition tracking.
Do residents need to travel to train with Konfide In One?+
No. All coaching and services are delivered on-site at the building gym. The coaching is tailored to each resident's goals and fitness level, starting with a functional movement assessment.
How is Konfide In One different from a standard personal trainer?+
KN1 uses a systematized methodology built around four pillars — Train, Fuel, Restore and Measure. Every engagement begins with a functional assessment, programming is science-backed, and nutrition and recovery are integrated from day one rather than treated as afterthoughts.
How do I bring Konfide In One to my building?+
Book a consultation through the Get Started page. We will discuss your property, resident demographic and goals, and design an on-site wellness program for your community.
